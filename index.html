Risk Controls

Critical Mitigation Strategies We Consult On

Based on our extensive experience in the grain storage sector, we provide consulting and evaluation services in the following key areas:

Supplier Qualification Program

Comprehensive supplier assessment criteria, pre-shipment inspections, certificate of analysis requirements, and performance scorecards to ensure only quality grain enters SABIL facilities.

Grain Monitoring Evaluation

Evaluate the effectiveness of existing grain monitoring systems for temperature, humidity, and quality control to ensure optimal storage conditions and prevent degradation.

Laboratory Quality Assurance

Assessment of calibration programs, proficiency testing participation, certified reference materials, and method validation to ensure accurate and reliable quality testing.

Centralized Calibration Oversight

Unified calibration system monitoring across all locations, ensuring consistent measurement accuracy, traceability to national standards, and efficient coordination from a central point.

Pest Management Evaluation

Evaluate the effectiveness of existing pest management systems, monitoring protocols, and control measures to ensure compliance and protection of stored grain.

Occupational Health & Safety Evaluation

Comprehensive evaluation of existing OHS systems, hazard identification processes, risk controls, and worker protection measures against ISO 45001 requirements.

Environmental Controls Assessment

Assessment of environmental management controls within ISO 14001 framework, including emissions, waste management, and compliance with Ministry of Environment requirements.

Traceability Consulting

Consulting on traceability practices including batch coding, chain of custody documentation, and recall procedures to meet SFDA and international requirements.

Emergency Response Plans

Elaborate or evaluate location-specific emergency procedures, evacuation protocols, first aid capabilities, and coordination with local emergency services.
